Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Deutsch

In short

The Levanh app does not collect personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics. The app itself does not connect to any server; for purchases and updates it only talks to the Google Play app or the App Store on your device. Everything you enter and all photos you add stay on your device. We only have data about you if you buy Levanh Premium (order data from Google Play or sales reports from Apple) or write to us. Both are described below.

What the app stores

The app stores on your device what you enter yourself: your starters (name, flour, hydration, jar weight), feedings with times, amounts (weighed or estimated), temperatures, observations and notes, bakes with recipes, ratings and notes, your saved own recipes, photos you add, the times under "My times", and your settings (for example your fridge temperature and how you weigh when feeding).

  • The data is kept in the app's storage on your device. Only you have access to it.
  • It is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • If you delete the app, the data is deleted too.
  • The app does not back up to any cloud by itself. On Android, the automatic backup to your Google account is turned off for Levanh.
  • Backups of your whole device that you set up in the operating system (for example an iPhone's iCloud backup) may contain the app's data. You control this in your device settings.

My times

Under "My times" you can choose to enter when you sleep or are usually away, for example "University, Mon–Fri 8–16", also with a name you pick yourself. Levanh only uses these times on your device to fit the bake plan around them. Like all other data, they are stored only on your device, not transmitted, and included in your backup file. Keep in mind: anyone who gets your backup file can read your daily routine from it. The app deletes one-off times once they are over. You can change or delete any time at any moment.

Backup file

If you choose "Back up my data" in the settings, the app creates a file with your data and opens your device's share menu. You decide where the file goes (for example a cloud drive or an email). The terms of that service apply there. When restoring, the app only reads the file you pick.

Photos

You can add photos to feedings, bakes and the growing diary. This is optional. To take a photo, Levanh opens your device's camera app; to choose one, it opens the system photo picker. Levanh only receives the photos you take or pick and has no access to your other photos. Photos are stored only in the app's storage on your device and included in your backup file. They are never uploaded. When you delete a photo, a starter or the app, the related photos are deleted.

Sharing a picture

When you share a bake, your starter, a collage or a recap as a picture, the app creates the picture on your device and opens your device's share menu. You decide where it goes. Levanh uploads nothing.

Feedings as a table

When you choose "Share as table (CSV)" in a starter's history, the app creates a table of that starter's feedings on your device (times, amounts, temperatures, flour type, forecast and observed peak, your notes) and opens your device's share menu. You decide where the file goes; the terms of that provider apply there. Levanh uploads nothing.

Feedback

The settings have a "Send feedback" item. It opens the page levanh.com/feedback in a browser window. If you turn it on in the settings, you can also shake your phone instead; the app then asks whether you want to send feedback. For this, the app evaluates the motion only on your device while it is open. None of it is stored or transmitted. Shaking is off by default and can be turned off again at any time. Levanh itself transmits nothing. You decide what you write into the form. It is processed through the website levanh.com, under the privacy policy published there.

Purchases (Levanh Premium)

You can buy Levanh Premium on Android through Google Play and on iPhone and iPad through Apple's App Store, as a one-time purchase or as a subscription. The subscription is available monthly or yearly; for the subscriptions, the store may offer a free trial. The store decides whether you are offered a trial based on your Google account or Apple ID (for example, whether you have had a subscription to this app before); the app only learns which offers are available to you. Google or Apple handles the purchase, payment, trial, renewal and cancellation. Google or Apple is your contract partner for this and is itself responsible for this processing; Google's or Apple's privacy policy applies.

We never receive payment data. For purchases through Google Play, in the Google Play Console we see for each purchase the order number, date, product, price, status and country (sometimes also region or postcode), but not your name or payment method. We can also find an order there by email address if you give it to us, for example for a refund. We use this information to handle purchases, process refunds and keep our accounts (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR). It is kept as long as the law requires, usually 10 years (German tax and commercial law, § 147 AO, § 257 HGB).

For purchases through the App Store, Apple only gives us summary sales and payment reports in App Store Connect (for example product, country, number of units and proceeds), but no information about individual buyers such as name, email address or payment method. You request refunds directly from Apple. We use these reports to keep our accounts (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) and keep them as long as the law requires.

The app only stores on your device whether Premium is unlocked, how (one-time purchase or subscription) and when it last checked, and checks this with Google Play or the App Store at start. "Redeem a code" and "Manage subscription" open Google Play or Apple's subscription settings.

Contact by email

If you write to us, we process your email address and the content of your message in order to reply. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if it concerns a purchase, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering enquiries). We delete the message once the matter is settled, unless statutory retention obligations require otherwise.

Updates

On Android, the app asks the Google Play app on your device at start whether there is a new version. Google Play handles checking, downloading and installing; Google's privacy policy applies. Levanh itself transmits nothing. On your device, the app only remembers for which version and when you dismissed an update notice. This is not included in the backup file.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled and shown on your device. There is no push service and no server. Allowing notifications is optional; without it the app works, just without reminders.

Widget and quick actions

The home screen widget, the quick actions on the app icon and the optional status bar notification show your starters' names and times. This information stays on your device, but anyone who sees your screen can read it.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): for reminders.
  • Start after the device restarts (Android): so scheduled reminders are kept.
  • Purchases through Google Play (Android): for Levanh Premium. On iPhone and iPad, purchases through the App Store need no separate permission.

In addition, system components for reminders and the widget bring technical permissions (vibration, briefly keeping the device awake, checking the network state, background tasks). They give the app no access to your data and no internet connection.

The app itself does not connect to the internet and does not use your location or your contacts. For photos it uses your device's camera app and photo picker, without its own camera or photo permission.

App stores

If you download Levanh from Google Play or the App Store, Google or Apple process data under their own privacy policies. From them we receive at most summary statistics that do not identify anyone, such as install numbers or anonymous crash reports if you allowed sharing them on your device.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). We do not store any data about you through the app itself; you can change or delete your data in the app at any time. For order data and emails we hold, contact us at the email address above. Statutory retention obligations may prevent deletion; in that case we restrict the processing.

Where we process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

Changes

If the app changes in a way that makes this policy inaccurate, we will update it. The current version is always in the app under Settings and at https://levanh.com.

GitHub decides how long the log files are kept. GitHub does not state a fixed period. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. Details are in GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact by phone

If you call me, I process your phone number and the content of the call in order to handle your request. The same applies as in the section "Contact by email".

Feedback form

At levanh.com/feedback you can send me feedback on the app. The form is run by the service Tally (Tally BV, Sint-Pietersnieuwstraat 11, 9000 Ghent, Belgium).

  • Fields: a screenshot (image upload), a description of what you noticed, and details for follow-up questions, for example your name or email address.
  • Purpose: evaluating feedback on the app and improving the app.
  • Voluntary: Using the form is voluntary. Required fields are marked in the form. The details for follow-up questions are optional; without them I do not know who sent the feedback. Instead of the form you can also email me; the section "Contact by email" then applies.

The form is only loaded when you click "Formular laden" (load form). Only then is data transferred to Tally, for example your IP address and information about your browser. Tally stores entries in your browser's storage to link your input to a form session. According to my check, the form does not set cookies. When the form loads, your browser also connects to Sentry, an error monitoring service used by Tally (Functional Software, Inc., USA). Your IP address may be transferred to the USA in the process.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; for the entries in your browser's storage, § 25(1) TDDDG), which you give by clicking "Formular laden" and submitting the form. You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future by emailing contact@levanh.com.

Tally processes the data on my behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). According to Tally, form data is stored encrypted in the EU. I delete a piece of feedback once I have evaluated and dealt with it. According to Tally, deleted data is also removed from its backups within 90 days at the latest.

Cookies, fonts and embedded services

This website does not set cookies, does not store anything in your browser's storage itself, and uses no analytics or tracking tools and no advertising. No content from other providers is embedded, except the feedback form, which is only loaded after you click. The fonts (Lora and Inter) are loaded directly from levanh.com. There is no connection to Google Fonts or any other font provider.

Links to other websites, for example to GitHub or Tally, are only opened when you click them. The privacy policy of the respective provider applies there.
