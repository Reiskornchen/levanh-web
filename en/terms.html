Estimates

The times in Levanh are estimates based on rules of thumb. Every starter, flour and kitchen is different. When feeding and baking, rely on what you see, smell and feel, not on the app alone.

Food safety

Levanh does not replace your own judgement of your food. Throw a starter or dough away if it shows mould, pink or orange streaks, or smells rotten. If in doubt, do not eat it.
